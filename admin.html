Basis of preparation

How every figure is produced, and what it can’t tell you. Targets come from admin-targets.json.

General basis
  • Source. Events recorded by dn-track.js on every page (website and app) and stored in the digi Supabase database (London).
  • Nightly copy. Players & growth uses the events directly. The Puzzles scorecard, worst puzzles, by band and yesterday’s flags read a copy of the page-by-page figures made at 00:20 each night, so they run to the end of yesterday. Refresh in Settings makes a new copy first.
  • Player. One browser or one app install, identified by a random code stored on that device, that started a puzzle. The same person on a phone and a laptop counts as two players. No names, emails or IP addresses are held.
  • Dates. UK dates (Europe/London). Ranges include both ends and end yesterday. “All” starts on 26 Sep 2026, when recording began.
  • Not counted. Visitors who switched recording off, browsers sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, browsers that blocked the script, visits to this admin page, and devices on the exclusion list (0 at present; exclude the device you’re on in Settings).
  • Bots. Browsers that say they are automated send nothing. Crawlers that hide it still get through; they rarely start a puzzle, so player figures are barely touched, but visitor figures under Other leave one-page visitors out unless switched on in Settings.
  • Small numbers. A type needs 20 attempts in the period before it gets a verdict, and a puzzle 5 players before it is flagged. Percentages from a handful of players swing wildly.
  • Retention. Raw events are kept for 13 months, then reduced to daily totals.
