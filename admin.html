Basis of preparation

How every figure is produced, and what it can’t tell you.

General basis
  • Source. Events recorded by dn-track.js on every page of the site and stored in the digi Supabase database (London). Figures are worked out live when this page loads or you press Refresh, so a change to a rule also applies to past dates.
  • Reader. One browser on one device, identified by a random code stored in that browser. The same person on a phone and a laptop counts as two readers; clearing browser data or a private window starts a new reader. No names, emails or IP addresses are held.
  • Visit. A run of activity by one reader with no gap longer than 30 minutes.
  • Page load. One opening of one page. Reloading, or returning via the back button after the page was discarded, counts again.
  • Dates. UK dates (Europe/London). A range includes both its start and end date. The 7, 30 and 90-day ranges end yesterday; today is shown on the daily chart as “so far”. “All” starts on 26 Sep 2026, when recording began.
  • Compared with. The same number of days just before the range. Not shown for All, or when the earlier days fall before recording began.
  • News list. The News list is also the home page, so opening it counts as the site, not as reading News (from 20 Oct 2026, and for past dates too). Taps on its cards still count as News list taps.
  • Not counted. Readers who switched recording off, browsers sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, readers whose browser blocked the script, visits to this admin page, and any browser on the exclusion list (0 at present, e.g. your own phone; exclude the device you’re on in Settings).
  • Losses. Events are sent in small batches. Some are lost when a phone closes a page abruptly or goes offline, most often the “left the page” event that carries time on page. Timings are averaged over page loads where that event arrived; loads without it are left out of those averages rather than counted as zero.
  • Bots. Browsers that say they are automated (the webdriver flag, or a crawler or headless user agent) send nothing, from 19 Oct 2026. Crawlers that hide it still get through, so one-page readers are left out of every figure by default; the switch in Settings brings them back in. Treat sudden spikes with care.
  • Small numbers. Percentages from a handful of readers swing wildly. “Worth a look” ignores anything with fewer than 5 readers or starts.
  • Retention. Raw events are kept for 13 months, then reduced to daily totals; ranges older than that will read as empty here.
