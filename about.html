Nine small puzzles, new every morning

The Daily Noodle is a free set of nine daily puzzles in British English. Each takes a few minutes, everyone gets the same ones, and there are no adverts.

Play today’s puzzles

The nine

  • MiniA small crossword
  • DokuA crossword with no clues
  • SweepMinesweeper you can always solve without guessing
  • PathOne unbroken trail from 1 to 36
  • LinkFour hidden groups of four words
  • TangleA 90-second word grid
  • BordersName the country from its outline
  • ReckonEstimate ten numbers
  • TallySudoku with a maths twist

What makes it different

  • Fair. Doku, Sweep, Path and Tally have one answer each and never need a guess.
  • The same for everyone. One set a day, so you can compare results with the people you know.
  • British English. Spellings, clues and general knowledge are written for players in the UK.
  • No adverts and no account. Sign in with your email only if you want your results on every device.
  • Leagues. A private daily leaderboard for family, friends or colleagues.
  • Kept for you. Streaks and personal bests for every puzzle, and an archive of every past day.

More than puzzles

The site also carries Noodle News, sourced data journalism with the figures shown, and a room of quick browser games.

On your phone

The Daily Noodle works in any browser, and you can add it to your home screen. An Android app, Daily Noodle, is in testing.

Contact

Questions, corrections and ideas are welcome at contact@dailynoodle.co.uk. How visits are counted, and how to switch that off, is on the privacy page.
