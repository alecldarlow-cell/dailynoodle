What we record, and how to stop it

We count how the site is used so we can make it better. We never know who you are.

What is recorded

  • Which pages, puzzles and games are opened, which card on a front page was tapped, and how long a page stays open.
  • For articles: how far down you scrolled, which charts came into view, and the website names of any sources you opened.
  • For puzzles: when you start and finish, whether you solved it, the time taken, and how many checks, hints or reveals you used.
  • For games: when a round starts and ends, whether you won, and how far you got.
  • The website you came from (its name only), whether you're on a phone, tablet or computer, and whether you opened the site from a home-screen icon.

To tell one visit from the next, your browser keeps a random code. It is not linked to your name, email or anything else about you.

What is never recorded

Your name, email address, IP address, precise location, what you type, or your puzzle answers. (The one exception, for leaderboards, is below.) There is no advertising and no data is sold or shared with anyone else.

Leaderboards (only if you join a league)

Leagues are private groups with a daily leaderboard for each puzzle. Nothing here happens unless you start or join one.

  • When you join, your browser keeps a second random code, separate from the one above, that links you to your leagues. Switching off recording doesn't remove you from a league.
  • Your nickname and your results for each puzzle (solved or not, time, score or mistakes) are shown to everyone in your leagues. Anyone with a league's join link can join it, so share it only with people you'd show your results to.
  • When you're in a league on a browser, your earlier results are copied onto the boards: the finished puzzles that browser remembers and, unless you'd switched recording off, its earlier finishes in the visit records above (including those from the site's old address, if you came across from it on that browser). This happens again if the browser later has more finished puzzles to add. The visit records' random code is used only for that copy and isn't kept with your nickname.
  • You can leave a league from its settings, and its owner can remove players, close it, rename it and change its join code. Leaving, removal and closing take you or the league off the boards; the records themselves are kept, hidden, not deleted.
  • The site's admin can see league names and nicknames.
  • To stop abuse, when you start or join a league a scrambled form of your network address is kept with the time. It can't be turned back into the address.
  • To have your nickname and results deleted rather than hidden, use the contact details below and give your league and nickname.

Where it is kept

On a Supabase database in London. Detailed records are kept for 13 months, then reduced to daily totals.

Switch it off

One tap stops all recording on this browser and deletes its random code. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, recording is already off.

Your rights and contact

Because we don't know who you are, we usually can't find records that belong to you. If you have a question or complaint, email CONTACT_EMAIL. You can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
