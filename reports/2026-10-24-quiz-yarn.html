How to play

Each level is one board and 90 seconds. Drag through touching letters, across, up, down or diagonally, to spell a word, or tap letters one by one and tap the last one again to enter. Each tile is used once per word; QU counts as two letters.

A word scores its length minus two: 1 point for three letters, 2 for four, 3 for five. The board’s longest common word scores double. Any real word counts, plurals included: rarer words score just the same.

The bar above the board shows the level’s five star scores. One star clears the level, knits a stripe into your scarf and opens the next. Replay any level for more stars.

Words you find here and in the daily Tangle all count towards your words found.
