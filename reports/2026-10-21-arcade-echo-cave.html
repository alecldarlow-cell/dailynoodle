A little light in the dark

Listen.
Look. Leave.

1

Drag to walk. Your lamp lights the ground beside you. Places you walk stay faintly outlined. Arrow keys or WASD work too.

2

Tap Echo to see further. Sound travels through passages, revealing the cave for a few seconds. It recharges in three seconds. Space works too.

3

Something is listening. Pale creatures investigate the place where you made a sound. Echo, then quietly slip away. Nearby footsteps also have a written warning.

4

Find the gold relic, then the exit. Small crystals are optional. You have three hearts and as much thinking time as you need.

Three reasons to return

★ Relic recovered and safely out.
★★ Every crystal collected too.
★★★ All of that without losing a heart.
