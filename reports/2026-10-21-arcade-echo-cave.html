A little light in the dark

Listen.
Look. Leave.

1

Drag to walk. Your lamp lights the ground beside you. Places you walk stay faintly outlined. Arrow keys or WASD work too.

2

Tap Echo to see further. Sound travels through passages, revealing the cave for a few seconds. It recharges in three seconds. Space works too.

3

Something is listening. Pale creatures investigate the place where you made a sound. Echo, then slip away. If a creature enters your lamplight with a clear view, it spots you and chases after a short warning. Turn a corner to break sight, then lure it away with another echo. Nearby footsteps also have a written warning.

4

Find the gold relic, then the exit. Tap Leave cave at the exit when you are ready. Small crystals are optional. You have three hearts and as much thinking time as you need.

Three reasons to return

One star: relic recovered and safely out.
Two stars: every crystal collected too.
Three stars: all of that without losing a heart.

Open the map to see your explored route and discovered landmarks. The cave pauses while you look. There is no penalty for using echoes. The daily cave is the same for everyone on the same London date.
