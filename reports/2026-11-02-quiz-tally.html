Tally

A numbers puzzle to try out. Ten boards; your best time for each is kept on this phone.

Fill every row and column with 1 to 5, no repeats.

Each heavy-bordered cage must make its number with the sign shown. The number is the answer, the sign is how you get there: 12× multiplies to 12, 9+ adds to 9, 5÷ is larger over smaller, 2− larger minus smaller. A lone number is that digit.

Every board is solved by logic alone. No guessing is ever needed.
