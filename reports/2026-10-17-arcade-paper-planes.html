Drag back and release to throw. In flight, hold the right side to raise the flaps (nose up) and the left side to lower them (nose down); let go and they go flat. Left alone the plane swoops up and down and glides steeply, so smooth it out: flaps down as it climbs, flaps up as it dives. Pulling up when slow stalls the wing; dive a little to get speed back. A steep throw plus a hold on the right makes a loop. ☰ pauses. Keyboard: ←/→ aim, Space throws; hold ↑ or Space for flaps up, ↓ for flaps down; P or Esc pauses. Clears keep your throw; 2× target restores one (maximum five).
