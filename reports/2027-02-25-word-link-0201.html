How to play

Find the four groups of four. Every tile belongs to exactly one group, but several are dressed as members of another — that's the whole puzzle. Pick four, then Submit. Get three right and you'll be told you're one away. Four mistakes ends it, and guessing the same four twice costs nothing. Groups run easiest to hardest: green, cobalt, marigold, ink.
