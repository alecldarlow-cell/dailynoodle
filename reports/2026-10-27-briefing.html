3 · Society

English housebuilding starts are up 20% on a year ago, but permissions are down 12%

Builders in England started 35,910 homes between April and June, up 20% on a year earlier. Completions were 35,800, up 1% on the year. Permission was granted for 212,000 homes in the year to June, down from 242,000.

Why it matters: Permissions feed future building, so fewer of them make the 1.5 million homes target harder to reach. That matters most to renters and first-time buyers.

Sources: Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government
